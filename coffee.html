BEGIN BREWING MODERN INDIAN COFFEE AT HOME

These brew guides serve as a good place to begin brewing speciality coffee at home.
The grinder and grind size you use, coffee you choose and recipe ofcourse, everything plays a important role.
Every coffee and its brewing technique is different, so we have listed a standard recipe in the most simplest video form.
Play around with your brewer and parameters to get each coffee to taste just the way you like it.

COLD BREW

Steeped slowly in cold water for 14 to 18 hours, this method yields a smooth, naturally sweet brew with exceptionally low acidity.

Perfect for warm days. Use a coarse grind and filtered water, then serve over ice for a refreshing, bold coffee experience.

FRENCH PRESS

A classic immersion method that produces a heavy-bodied, rich cup. The metal mesh filter allows natural coffee oils to shine.

Use a coarse grind and steep in hot water for exactly four minutes. Press down slowly to extract deep, robust flavors.

AEROPRESS

A versatile and rapid brewing device that uses gentle air pressure to extract a clean, smooth, and highly concentrated cup.

Highly portable and easy to clean. Experiment with inverted methods, fine grinds, and varying water temperatures to perfect your brew.
